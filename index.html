Manual inputs (optional)

Binance can't be reached from this page. Funding is scored live from the gap between Crypto.com's ETH perpetual and the spot index: +0.05% or more is crowded longs (short +5), −0.05% or less is long +5. Type a Binance funding rate to override it. No free connector offers a long/short account ratio, so that box stays manual. Typed values are saved in this browser only. Typed funding of 0.01% or more, or 60%+ long accounts, counts as short +5; negative funding or 40% or fewer long accounts counts as long +5.

Method: support is the lowest 4H low of the last ~8 days; resistance is the nearest 4H swing high above price. EMA stack uses 10/20/50 because the feed returns 50 candles, not enough for EMA 100. Taker flow uses the last 150 trades as a stand-in for CVD. Liquidity pools are untouched 4H and 1D swing highs and lows, where stop orders usually sit; a real liquidation heatmap is not available from any free feed. The order book feed only goes 50 levels deep, so walls are shown for the price right next to market. This is a rule-based read of the data, not financial advice.
