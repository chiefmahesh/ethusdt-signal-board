Manual inputs (optional)

Binance can't be reached from this page. Funding is scored live from the gap between Crypto.com's ETH perpetual and the spot index: +0.05% or more is crowded longs (short +5), −0.05% or less is long +5. Type a Binance funding rate to override it. No free connector offers a long/short account ratio, so that box stays manual. Typed values are saved in this browser only. Typed funding of 0.01% or more, or 60%+ long accounts, counts as short +5; negative funding or 40% or fewer long accounts counts as long +5.

Data source: opened inside claude.ai the page uses the Crypto.com connector; opened as a downloaded file in your browser it pulls Binance directly (spot, plus funding, open interest and the long/short account ratio from Binance Futures). Method: support is the lowest 4H low of the last ~8 days; resistance is the nearest 4H swing high above price. The EMA stack adds EMA 100 when enough candles are available (Binance). Taker flow uses recent trades as a stand-in for CVD. Liquidity pools are untouched 4H and 1D swing highs and lows, where stop orders usually sit; a real liquidation heatmap is not available from any free feed. Order book walls are limited to the depth each feed returns. This is a rule-based read of the data, not financial advice.
